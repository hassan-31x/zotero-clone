# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Small research teams of 2–15 people reading technical papers at a desk, often for extended sessions, and moving between source material and their own understanding.
## Product Purpose
Import papers into a private or shared library, read the original PDF, annotate evidence, build notes, and study with a paper-grounded assistant. The source stays first-class and accessible without AI.
## Brand Personality
Simple, minimal, clean, modern. Margin is calm research software with a familiar, Overleaf-inspired split workspace and predictable controls.
## Anti-references
Generic AI dashboard cards, oversized workspace heroes, decorative gradients, simulated papers and invented research results.
## Design Principles
Keep the original source visible. Make the next action obvious. Preserve context across sessions. Use real data and explicit empty states. Put advanced controls behind progressive disclosure.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-friendly navigation, labeled controls, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive pane switching and reduced-motion support, as required by the product specification.
